Features

Everything between this round and your next win

Every ballot tells you something. RoundReview makes sure you hear it.

Judge Feedback Journal

Save every ballot with the tournament, round, judge, result, speaker points, and improvement tags. Search and filter your entire season in seconds.

Tournament Calendar

Plan your season with statuses from Planning to Completed, registration deadlines, and a live countdown to your next competition.

Performance Analytics

Win rate, speaker point trends, side performance, and tournament-by-tournament records — all charted automatically from your rounds.

Improvement Insights

See which areas judges mention most, where you're strongest, and exactly what to practice before the next tournament.

Debate Timer

Preset speech times for LD, Public Forum, Policy, and Parliamentary — plus custom formats, prep tracking, and a final-30-seconds warning.

Pre-Tournament Checklist

Evidence, logistics, tech, personal items, and mental prep — organized, checkable, and saved so nothing gets forgotten on tournament morning.
